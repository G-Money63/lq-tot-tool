Monthly close — step by step

Your full monthly routine, from downloading the Guesty report to paying La Quinta the tax. Work top to bottom.

Phase 1 · Get your two files
1In Guesty Pro, export the all-listings Reservations CSV for the month.
2From Morgan Stanley, download the LQRM bank statement PDF for the same month.
Phase 2 · Load them in
3Open the tool → Home → drop the Guesty CSV in box 1 and the bank PDF in box 2.
4The status cards fill in (units, reservations, tax due, needs review, exempt, TOT-only).
Phase 3 · City tax review
5Go to City tax. Any REVIEW row needs a decision — tap G (Guesty base) or A (Airbnb base), or type the correct rent. The flag clears once you choose.
6Check each unit’s tier:
≤26 nights Standard 11% form (10% TOT + 1% TBID).
28–29 nights No TBID due — 10% TOT only, on a Non-TBID TOT form requested from vacationlq@laquintaca.gov.
30+ nights Exempt — file a zero return (0 on lines 1–3).
Phase 4 · Match the money
7Go to Revenue match. Fix any deposit that didn’t auto-match (split it, or type a prior-month name) until Unallocated = $0.
Phase 5 · Enter expenses
8Go to Expenses. Add each unit’s costs (Turno cleaning, repairs) and set Paid by — “Owner’s bank” or “You (charge owner)”.
Phase 6 · Owner statements
9Go to Owner reports. Check the numbers → View, then Print/PDF or Email each statement. Print all for your records.
Phase 7 · File & pay the city
10Go to laquinta.munirevs.com → Business Center. File and pay each unit ($1 ACH). Standard form for ≤27-night stays; Non-TBID TOT form for 28–29; zero return for 30+.
Deadline: file and pay by the end of the following month. (Confirm against MuniRevs for your units.)
Phase 8 · Settle owner money
11Deposit each owner’s distribution to their account.
12From the owner’s account, pay the city tax + owner-paid expenses.
13Send each owner their statement.
Key contacts: MuniRevs — laquinta.munirevs.com/business-center · City — vacationlq@laquintaca.gov · Bank — Morgan Stanley (La Quinta Resort Management, LLC).
